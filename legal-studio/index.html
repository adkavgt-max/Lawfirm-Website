Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. I work with Nepali families and businesses living abroad on matters connected to Nepal. We'll agree how to communicate and handle documents before we begin.

How much does a first consultation cost?

The introductory 15-minute call is free. If more time or legal work is needed, I'll explain the fee and scope before you decide.

Which languages do you work in?

I can communicate in Nepali and English. Please tell me your preferred language when you get in touch.

Do you only work with companies?

No. I also help individuals and families with selected property and dispute matters connected to Nepal.

How do your fees work?

Each matter is different. You'll receive a clear scope and fee before any paid work begins. Monthly retainers are available for ongoing support.

Does contacting you make me a client?

No. A lawyer-client relationship begins only after we both agree to work together and confirm the terms in writing.
